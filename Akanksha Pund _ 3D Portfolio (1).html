About me

I like knowing why a thing works.

I'm an Electronics and Telecommunication student who enjoys the space between a sensor reading and a decision. A signal on a wire, a row in a database, an alert on a screen: I want to understand each step and build it myself.

My projects connect hardware and software: ESP32 and Arduino boards, health sensors, a robotic sorting arm, and a battery data pipeline in Python. PCB design and AI/ML are what I'm exploring next.

Project 1

Saline Monitoring System

An ESP32-based patient assistance system pairing sensor monitoring with a web interface. It tracks temperature and ECG, raises alerts and offers assistance controls so a caregiver can respond sooner.

3-member team. 1st Prize at project competitions at Deogiri College and MIT.

  • ESP32
  • Sensors
  • Web interface
  • IoT

Project 2

IoT smart health monitoring

An Arduino Mega 2560 system monitoring ECG, temperature and falls with AD8232, LM35 and ADXL sensors. A flex-sensor-operated APR33A3 voice module and a Node.js portal that updates records every 3 hours complete it.

4-member team. Demonstrated at K. K. Wagh Institute of Engineering Education and Research and at Takshyantra, Shreeyash College of Engineering.

  • Arduino Mega 2560
  • AD8232
  • LM35
  • ADXL
  • APR33A3
  • Node.js

Project 3

Garlic impurity detection and sorting

An ESP32 and Python solution that identifies paper, stones and other unwanted material on a conveyor line. A 3-axis delta robotic arm sorts, and Blynk controls the conveyor and its speed.

3-member team. 2nd place at Idea Storm, Yuva Verse 2.0.

  • ESP32
  • Python
  • Delta arm
  • Blynk

Project 4

Battery management and aging analysis

My part: understanding the NASA battery datasets, cleaning and preprocessing them, extracting readings into CSV files, and storing readings and analysis history in SQLite for a backend. The dashboard shows capacity, voltage and temperature trends across discharge cycles. The team secured 2nd prize.

  • Python
  • Pandas
  • SciPy
  • SQLite
  • CSV

AI/ML prediction of battery condition is the proposed next stage. It is not implemented yet.
